# Visual Impairment Support

I made this mod for a friend of mine who plays Valheim and has significant vision impairment due to the loss of part of his optic nerve.

## Current Feature

The mod adds a red outline to inventory slots containing weapons, armor, or tools. Shields, utility items, and ammunition are highlighted as well. Other items are left unoutlined. This is a visual change only; it does not affect item behavior or properties.

## Why I Made This Mod

My friend would often throw away equipment because he mistook it for ordinary loot that should be discarded. The outlines make it easier to spot which items in his inventory are important and worth keeping.

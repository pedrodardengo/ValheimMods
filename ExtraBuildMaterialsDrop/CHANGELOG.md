# Changelog

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-10

### Changed

- Restricted the multiplier to wood and stone drops from trees and rocks.
- Made the configured multiplier override the world's resource modifier for those materials.
- Restricted the multiplier choices to 1x through 3x in 0.5x increments.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-10

### Added

- Added a configurable multiplier for wood and stone drop quantities.
- Applied the multiplier after Valheim's world drop modifier, without changing other item drops.

# True Weather Swamp

Clear skies in the Swamp two-thirds of the time. The other third, rain just like it used to.

True Weather Swamp is not about removing rain from the Swamp. Rain is part of what makes the biome feel like the Swamp, and it still has a 33% chance of raining. For comparison, the chance of rain in the Meadows is 9%.

But when it rains all the time, the Swamp can start to feel one-note. The rain makes it harder to see, and can make the buildings you worked on feel less cozy. The Swamp is a rich, beautiful biome in both kinds of weather: its rainy atmosphere has its own charm, and clear skies reveal a different side of it. Now you can admire your builds in the Swamp without taking away the rain that belongs there.
